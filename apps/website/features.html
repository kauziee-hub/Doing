A request. A little less to do.

One private assistant for your PC, browser and meetings.

On your PC

Find a file, open an app, adjust volume or brightness, or change a setting. Choose Allow app control to let Doing read an app’s accessible controls, click, type and scroll for this session.

  • Search for files across Windows Search and your personal folders.
  • Work with accessible app controls, including music searches, settings and messages. Say explicitly when to send or save.
  • Allow a folder for text and code edits, with session Undo. Deleted items go to Windows Recycle Bin.

Some apps do not expose usable controls. Doing stops when it cannot confirm an operation. Passwords, payments, security settings and administrator prompts need your input.

In your browser

Open the Doing extension in Chrome or Edge. Allow a tab, then chat beside the page. Ask questions, fill forms, select choices, type code and continue through a workflow.

  • Questions and actions share one conversation.
  • Add up to eight allowed tabs and work across them.
  • Say when to submit or go next in your message. Otherwise Doing asks before advancing.
  • Completion replies and follow-up instructions stay in the side panel.

Standard page controls and common code editors are supported. Login, CAPTCHA, hidden content and some embedded editors need your help. Browser actions require the website to be reachable.

In meetings

Choose Meetings to transcribe a call after everyone has agreed. Your microphone and call audio are labelled separately. Ask for explanations, a summary, decisions or action items while the conversation continues.

A compact overlay and global shortcuts keep answers nearby. Press Ctrl Shift O to answer a question shown on your screen. Screen reading needs the NVIDIA setup. Audio and screenshots stay in memory.

Your context, when you need it

Add notes, PDFs, Word files, code or a personal profile in Library. Meeting answers can draw on that context. Browser chat uses the tabs and facts you supply; file chat uses the folder you allow.

History keeps meeting transcripts and answers only if you enable saving. Assistant and browser conversations stay in memory for the current app session.

Privacy & setup

No account, subscription, API keys, ads or analytics. Doing downloads and verifies its local speech and language models once, choosing the right size for your PC. Updates are optional.

Local processing keeps prompts away from cloud models. It does not make every action risk-free: files you create remain on disk, and browser submissions send information to the website. Review important results. See Privacy and the setup guide.
