What Doing does.

Speech recognition, a fast language model and your own documents, all on your computer.

Meetings, written down

Doing transcribes your microphone and what plays through your speakers as two separate streams, so you always know who said what: Meet in a browser tab, the Teams or Zoom app, a video, or a Bluetooth headset.

  • Text appears while people speak and is finalised when they pause.
  • In a room? One microphone can stand for everyone.
  • Ask “What did we decide?” for decisions, action items and open questions.
  • Audio is processed in memory and never saved.

Answers when you need them

Questions are answered as they come up, often before the speaker has finished. Ask out loud or type; there are no modes to pick.

You askYou get
About you or your workA first-person answer from your notes and résumé. If they don't cover it, Doing says so.
A coding or design taskThe approach, working code and its complexity.
“What is…”, “How does…”A plain explanation.
“Summarise”, “What did we decide?”Decisions and action items from the transcript.

One click makes an answer longer, simpler or shorter. Settings only set length, language and tone.

Your screen

Press Ctrl Shift O and Doing answers whatever question or task is on your screen, with nothing to copy or paste. It also looks when a question points at something shown, like “add role-based access to this”.

  • The picture stays in memory, goes only to the model on your PC and is discarded after the answer.
  • If what you ask about isn't on the screen, it says so instead of guessing.
  • Measured on an RTX 4060 laptop: first words 1.5–3.3 s after the question, capture included. Needs an NVIDIA graphics card.
  • A small model gets tricky code wrong sometimes: read it before you use it.

Prep and history

Add PDFs, Word files, notes or a résumé under Prep, with a short profile of yourself. Doing indexes them on your PC and answers from them. History keeps past transcripts and answers if you turn it on; it's off by default.

Overlay and shortcuts

A small translucent window floats above your apps and never takes focus. Drag it anywhere, make it compact or move it to another monitor. Every action has a shortcut; the full list is in the guide.

By default Windows is asked to leave Doing's windows out of screen shares and screenshots. Cameras and some remote-desktop tools ignore this, so treat it as a privacy convenience, not invisibility.

The engine

On first launch Doing checks your hardware and installs its own engine: whisper.cpp for speech and an Ollama runtime for answers, both on 127.0.0.1 only. With an NVIDIA graphics card (6 GB or more) it uses Whisper large-v3-turbo and Qwen 3.5 4B; otherwise Whisper small and Qwen 3 1.7B on the processor. See the requirements.

Privacy

No account, keys or analytics. Nothing you say, type or add is sent anywhere. The only internet use is the one-time engine download and an optional daily update check. Details on the privacy page.
