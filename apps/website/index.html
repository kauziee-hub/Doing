What it does

Everything a call needs, nothing it doesn't.

A transcript you can trust, answers when you need them and a summary when you ask. All of it in one quiet window.

Live transcript

Whisper turns speech into text while people talk, with your voice and the other side labelled separately.

Answers in about half a second

When a question comes up, a short, clear answer appears before you need it. Ask your own questions by typing, too.

Meeting notes

In Notes mode Hirora stays quiet during the call. Ask it any time for the decisions, action items and open questions so far, straight from the transcript.

Grounded in your documents

Add notes, specs, a job description or your résumé. Answers use what's in them and say so when something isn't.

Private by design

Speech recognition and the answer model run on your PC. No sign-up, no keys, no telemetry, and audio is never saved.

A quiet overlay

A small translucent window that stays on top, never steals focus and is left out of screen shares where Windows allows it.

The app

Built to stay out of your way.

Pick a mode, press Start, and keep talking. The overlay shows the transcript and answers; the dashboard keeps everything else.

Getting started

Up and running in three steps.

  1. Install

    Download the installer and run it. No admin rights needed.

  2. Set up once

    Press Set up Hirora. It checks your PC and downloads the right speech and answer models, verifying every file.

  3. Start listening

    Join your call, press Start and confirm everyone has agreed. Answers and notes appear in the overlay.

Use cases

One assistant, many kinds of calls.

Meetings and stand-ups

Follow every word, look up what someone just referred to, and ask for a summary of decisions and action items before the call ends.

Modes: Meeting Notes, General Q&A

Lectures and learning

Get a plain explanation of a term the moment it's mentioned, without pausing the class or the video.

Mode: Explain / Teach

Technical discussions

Paste an error or a snippet and get an explanation, working code and test cases you can copy.

Mode: Coding

Interview practice

Rehearse with mock interviews: add your résumé and the job description, and Hirora suggests answers built from your real experience, flagging anything your materials don't cover.

Modes: Interview Practice, Live Copilot

Use it where it's allowed. Many interviews and assessments don't permit AI help. Hirora is made for practice and for calls where assistance is permitted, and you should always tell people when you're transcribing them.
