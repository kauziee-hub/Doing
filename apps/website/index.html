How it works

Ready in three steps

  1. 1

    Install Hirora

    Run the installer (no admin rights needed) and paste the Hirora Cloud access code you were given. That's it: transcription and answers run on the free shared server, so any PC works, no GPU or paid key needed.

  2. 2

    Add your story

    Drop in your résumé, project notes and the job description. They're indexed on your PC, so answers about you quote your own words.

  3. 3

    Start listening

    Confirm everyone has agreed, press Start, and the overlay shows the transcript, the question it heard and a short cue to answer with.

Features

Built for the hard questions

Hears both sides, separately

A small native helper captures your microphone and your speakers as two labelled streams. The other side's question is detected and answered. No audio is ever saved to disk.

Won't invent your past

Questions about you are answered only from your materials, with citations. If a fact isn't there, it says “not in your materials”.

Coding rounds

Approach, runnable code, complexity and tests, with one-click copy, “explain simply” and “make it shorter”.

Practice mode

Rehearse with your own résumé before the real thing. Hirora cues you the way it would in a call.

A quiet overlay

See-through, always on top and never steals focus. Drag it anywhere, compact it, or jump monitors with a hotkey.

Run it locally

Answers from your own GPU

Optional, for PCs with an NVIDIA GPU. Choose Ollama in Settings and Hirora writes answers on your own machine: your questions never leave it, and it keeps working if the cloud is busy or offline (Hirora uses it automatically as a fallback when you set it).

Privacy

Private by default

  • Nothing is captured until you press Start and confirm everyone has agreed.
  • No Hirora server, no account, no telemetry. Audio goes only to the transcription provider you chose; questions only to your answer model.
  • History is off unless you turn it on, and you can delete it per session or all at once.
  • Screenshots and clipboard text are always previewed first, with likely secrets flagged for redaction.

About screen sharing

Hirora can ask Windows to leave the overlay out of screen captures. Some capture methods honour that and others never will (cameras, some remote-desktop tools). It's a privacy convenience, not invisibility. Follow the rules of any interview or assessment you take.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Hirora free?

Yes. There's no account or subscription. Hirora Cloud — a small always-on server — runs open models and Whisper speech-to-text on Groq's free tier, so everyone who has the access code uses it at no cost. The free tier has daily limits shared by everyone on the server (roughly 1,000 answers and a few hours of transcription a day). Prefer your own quota? Add your own Gemini or xAI key, or run answers on your own GPU with Ollama.

Is it allowed in interviews?

Often not. Many interviews and assessments forbid AI help, so check the rules first. Hirora is great for practice, for mock interviews, and for calls where assistance is permitted. Always tell people when you're transcribing them.

How fast are the answers?

Measured on our test laptop with a fresh install and only the access code: a typed question gets its first answer word in about half a second (median 0.55 s, 0.42–0.82 s over 35 questions). For spoken questions, Hirora Cloud transcribes each sentence when the speaker pauses — the text appeared 1.5–1.8 s after the question ended (two runs) — and the answer then started 0.6–0.7 s later. Speeds vary with the free tier's load; the Performance page in the app shows your own numbers.

Do I need an API key?

No — just the Hirora Cloud access code from whoever shared Hirora with you. Optional extras: your own Gemini or xAI key (transcription and answers on your own quota), DeepSeek or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint for answers, or Ollama on your own PC with no key at all.

Does it record my calls?

No. While you're listening, each spoken sentence is sent to the transcription service you chose (Hirora Cloud by default) and is never written to disk — on your PC or on the server. Transcript history is off unless you turn it on.

Why does Windows show a warning when I install?

The installer isn't code-signed yet. Compare the SHA-256 shown above with your downloaded file (PowerShell: Get-FileHash .\Hirora-Setup-*.exe). If they match, choose More info → Run anyway.
