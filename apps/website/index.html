What it does

Three things, done well.

Meetings, written down

A live transcript of any call or meeting, with you and the others labelled. Ask for the decisions and action items whenever you like.

Answers when you need them

Questions answered in about half a second, in plain words, from your notes, résumé and documents. Explanations, code and summaries alike.

Reads your screen

Press Ctrl Shift O and Doing answers whatever is on your screen: code, a slide, a question, nothing to copy or paste.

Private by design

Safe, because it never leaves your PC.

Local AI

Speech recognition and answers run on your computer. Nothing you say or show is sent anywhere.

No account, no tracking

Free, with no sign-up, keys, analytics or telemetry. Audio and screen captures are never saved.

Verified downloads

Every model and every update is checked against its published SHA-256 before it is used.

Getting started

Up and running in three steps.

  1. Install

    Download the installer and run it. No admin rights needed.

  2. Set up once

    Press Set up Doing. It checks your PC and downloads the right speech and answer models, verifying every file.

  3. Start listening

    Press Start and confirm everyone has agreed. The transcript and answers appear in a small overlay.
