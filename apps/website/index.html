What it does

Less busywork. More done.

Your PC, a helping hand

Find files, open apps and change a setting. Allow app control to click, type and scroll, or choose a folder for file edits with Undo.

Your browser, in conversation

Chat beside your tabs. Ask about a page, fill a form, select options or type code. Tell Doing when to submit and continue, then get a reply when the work is finished.

Your meetings, written down

Follow a live transcript, ask questions and pull out decisions and action items. Add your own documents for context, or ask about what is on your screen.

Private by design

Your AI runs here. Your access stays yours.

Local AI

Speech recognition and AI answers run on your computer. Your prompts and files are not sent to a cloud model.

No account, no tracking

Free, with no sign-up, keys, analytics or telemetry. Audio and screen captures are never saved.

Verified downloads

Every model and every update is checked against its published SHA-256 before it is used.

Getting started

Up and running in three steps.

  1. Install

    Download the installer and run it. No admin rights needed.

  2. Set up once

    Press Set up Doing. It checks your PC and downloads the right speech and answer models, verifying every file.

  3. Ask Doing

    Describe a file task in Assistant, open chat beside a Chrome tab, or start a meeting transcript.
