How it works

Ready in three steps

  1. 1

    Install and press Set up

    Run the installer (no admin rights needed). On first launch press Set up Hirora: it checks your PC and downloads its speech and answer engine once, verifying every file. No account, no keys.

  2. 2

    Add your story

    Drop in your résumé, project notes and the job description. They're indexed on your PC, so answers about you quote your own words.

  3. 3

    Start listening

    Confirm everyone has agreed, press Start, and the overlay shows the transcript, the question it heard and a short cue to answer with.

Features

Built for the hard questions

Hears both sides, separately

A small native helper captures your microphone and your speakers as two labelled streams. The other side's question is detected and answered. No audio is ever saved to disk.

Won't invent your past

Questions about you are answered only from your materials, with citations. If a fact isn't there, it says “not in your materials”.

Coding rounds

Approach, runnable code, complexity and tests, with one-click copy, “explain simply” and “make it shorter”.

Practice mode

Rehearse with your own résumé before the real thing. Hirora cues you the way it would in a call.

A quiet overlay

See-through, always on top and never steals focus. Drag it anywhere, compact it, or jump monitors with a hotkey.

Runs on your PC

One engine, set up for you

Hirora transcribes with Whisper and writes answers with Qwen, both running on your own computer. It picks the size that fits: the large models on an NVIDIA graphics card with 6 GB or more, smaller ones on the processor everywhere else. Nothing you say is sent anywhere, and it keeps working offline.

  • No NVIDIA GPU?It still runs, on the processor: answers started 2–4 s after a spoken question in our test (laptop on battery).
  • No keys, no serverThe only internet use is the one-time download from GitHub, Hugging Face and the Ollama library.

Privacy

Private by default

  • Nothing is captured until you press Start and confirm everyone has agreed.
  • No server, no account, no telemetry. Audio is transcribed and answers are written on your PC; nothing you say leaves it.
  • History is off unless you turn it on, and you can delete it per session or all at once.
  • Screenshots and clipboard text are always previewed first, with likely secrets flagged for redaction.

About screen sharing

By default Hirora asks Windows to leave the overlay out of screen captures, so you see it but a shared screen doesn't (measured with Chrome and Edge screen sharing). Some capture methods never honour that (cameras, some remote-desktop tools), and you can switch it off in Settings. It's a privacy convenience, not invisibility. Follow the rules of any interview or assessment you take.

What you need

Windows 11 (64-bit) and an internet connection for the one-time setup. With an NVIDIA graphics card (6 GB or more) Hirora downloads about 6.2 GB and answers fastest; on other PCs about 3.2 GB, and answers take longer (2–4 s in our test). Keep the laptop plugged in during calls.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Hirora free?

Yes. There's no account, subscription or key. Everything runs on your own PC, so there's no server to pay for and no usage limit.

Is it allowed in interviews?

Often not. Many interviews and assessments forbid AI help, so check the rules first. Hirora is great for practice, for mock interviews, and for calls where assistance is permitted. Always tell people when you're transcribing them.

How fast are the answers?

Measured on our RTX 4060 laptop, plugged in, with Hirora 0.3.0: a typed question gets its first answer word after a median 0.55 s (0.50–1.97 s over 12 questions), and Whisper transcribes a 2.4–3.5 s spoken question in 0.14–0.25 s. With spoken questions, when the question is clear before the speaker finishes, the answer is already written and appeared 0.3 s after they stopped; otherwise it started 1.2–2.7 s after. Press Start a minute before the call: the first question after starting waits about 15–20 s while the model loads. On battery the GPU slows down a lot, so keep the laptop plugged in.

What if my PC has no NVIDIA graphics card?

Hirora uses smaller models on the processor (Whisper small and Qwen 3 1.7B, about 3.2 GB). In our test with the GPU switched off and the laptop on battery, spoken questions were transcribed correctly and answers started 2.3–3.6 s after the speaker stopped. Answering screenshots needs the NVIDIA GPU setup; copy the text and use the clipboard instead.

What does the setup download?

Ollama's runtime (1.6 GB, from GitHub), whisper.cpp and a Whisper speech model (GitHub and Hugging Face), and the Qwen answer model from the Ollama library: about 6.2 GB with an NVIDIA card, 3.2 GB without. Every file is checked against its published SHA-256 before it is used. If you already use Ollama, Hirora reuses it and its models.

Does it record my calls?

No. Audio is transcribed on your PC while you listen and is never written to disk. Transcript history is off unless you turn it on.

Why does Windows show a warning when I install?

The installer isn't code-signed yet. Compare the SHA-256 shown above with your downloaded file (PowerShell: Get-FileHash .\Hirora-Setup-*.exe). If they match, choose More info → Run anyway.
